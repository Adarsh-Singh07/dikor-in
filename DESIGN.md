# DIKOR DESIGN SYSTEM & ATELIER IDENTITY
**Dikor.in | Bespoke 3D Pet & Human Miniatures**

---

## 1. Brand Soul & Purpose
DIKOR is an artisan 3D miniature studio based in Jalandhar, Punjab, founded by Rajat Saini and Sheetal. We sculpt and hand-paint custom 3D figurines exclusively for:
1. **Dogs** (Slim breeds and bulky breeds, 5" and 7" scales)
2. **Cats** (Indie and pedigree breeds, 5" and 7" scales)
3. **Humans** (Individual portraits, children, cherished milestones, 5" and 7" scales)

**Explicit Exclusion**: DIKOR does NOT sell religious idols, wedding couple plinths, or generic home decor. All catalogue items and configuration flows reflect only Dogs, Cats, and Humans.

### Visual & Emotional Tone
- **Tactile Atelier E-Commerce**: Product-first, editorial, grounded in physical craft (resin sculpture, hand acrylic detailing, brass engraving).
- **Anti-Slop Restraint**: Zero bento grids, zero card soup, zero floating gradient blobs, zero decorative sparkle glyphs, zero fake carousel cylinders.
- **Craftsman Precision**: Direct product customizer above the fold inspired by physical atelier studios like MakeDoppel.

## 4. Anti-Slop Architectural Rules
- **Rule R-02 (Zero Em Dashes)**: Absolutely no `—` anywhere in copy.
- **Rule R-03 (Mobile Responsiveness & 44px Tap Targets)**: Every button, toggle, and selector chip has a minimum touch target of 44px by 44px.
- **Rule R-05 (Zero Bento Grids / Card Soup)**: Layout is driven by real e-commerce product needs, not arbitrary card mosaics.
- **Rules R-17 & R-18 (Verbatim Authentic Proof Only)**: 100% verified customer quotes from `@dikor_in` Instagram archive with real pet names (Babu, Dido, Anna, Chivas, Gappu, Bruno).
- **Rule R-24 (Zero Dead Links)**: All navigation paths lead to live routes or verified WhatsApp/Instagram connections.
- **Rule R-25 & R-32 (Accessibility & Keyboard Control)**: Visible focus rings, accessible labels for all inputs, keyboard operable dialogs.
